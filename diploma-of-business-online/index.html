Studying the BSB50120 Diploma of Business online with 3CIR Online

If you already supervise a small team, coordinate projects in an office, or help run a small business, a Diploma-level qualification can put formal structure around the work you are already doing. The BSB50120 Diploma of Business is a nationally recognised AQF Level 5 qualification. training.gov.au describes it as reflecting business services roles, some with frontline management accountabilities, where people carry out moderately complex tasks in a specialist field.

Who it tends to suit

  • People who have been asked to step into frontline management
  • Administrators and coordinators who want to move into a management role
  • Small business owners who want recognised business skills behind their day-to-day decisions

How the course is put together

The qualification is made up of core units and electives. training.gov.au requires at least two electives from Group A (Business Operations), and if you choose four electives from one group, that group can be named as a specialisation on your testamur, for example Leadership, Compliance or Business Development. The units cover practical tasks such as managing budgets, planning resources, writing workplace policies and leading communication in a team.

With 3CIR Online you study online, at your own pace, and there are no exams. Course details, the unit list and enrolment requirements are on the course page at 3CIR Online.
